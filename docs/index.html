DRIVER'S BRIEFING

How to Play

Three laps. One canyon. Keep the wheels straight and the style high.

01 · The Line

Follow the glowing gate posts and the painted chevrons. Brake before the tight stuff, then get back on the throttle early. The dry riverbed is wide — use all of it.

02 · The Moves

  • Drift — hold the drift key through a corner. The longer you hold a clean slide, the higher the chain multiplier and the faster your boost refills.
  • Boost — burns the meter for a serious shove. Earn it back with drifts, clean landings, wall-rides and near-misses.
  • Air — kickers and gap jumps give you airtime. Land straight and you keep every bit of speed.
  • Wall-ride — skim a canyon wall at speed to spark and top up boost. Straight-on hits cost time.
